DISSONANCE

You're a music note, and you're alive.

Some notes have snuck into the song that don't belong in the key. They sound off, and they're coming for you. Shoot your own pitch at the red, off-key notes to knock them back into tune. Clear them all to finish the level.

Your shots only hit notes on the same line or space as you. Grab sharps ♯ to move up a step and flats ♭ to move down one. Don't shoot the gold notes. They belong in the key, and hitting them costs you harmony.
